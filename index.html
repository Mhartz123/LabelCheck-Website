How a verdict is decided
The app's rules, applied in order · the first that fires decides
  1. WARNINGThe label text matched an entry on the FDA Philippines advisory list (about 20,800 unregistered health products).
  2. NON-COMPLIANTExpired, no expiry printed, expiry photographed but unreadable, or no ingredient list. Also packaging damage with top confidence of 70% or more.
  3. COMPLIANTNone of the above. Detections below 70% are still drawn on the photos for review.
An unreadable value is never a pass. A damage check that could not run is reported as unavailable, which is neither damaged nor clean.
