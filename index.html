FDA Export Registration Compliance Update
All manufacturers listed in the LabelCheck database must finalize their 2024 Export Registration. Failure to update the "Compliance Registry Number" (CRN) by May 1st will result in automatic flagging of all product labels associated with the facility. Access the Partner Portal to submit documentation.
